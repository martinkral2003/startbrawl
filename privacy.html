Star Brawl – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Star Brawl (the "Game", available as a web game and as a mobile app) handles. The Game is operated by Martin Kral ("we", "us"). You can contact us at martin.kral.03@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no passwords.
  • We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • The Game contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • To play with others, the Game sends game data through a third-party public messaging server (see below).

Third-party services

  • Public MQTT broker (broker.emqx.io, operated by EMQ Technologies). Multiplayer messages pass through this public service. Anyone who knows or guesses a room code could observe the messages in that room. Do not share personal information in your player name. Their privacy policy applies to their handling of connection data: emqx.com/en/privacy-policy.
  • GitHub Pages (web version only). The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses as part of normal web hosting: GitHub Privacy Statement.

The mobile apps include all game files locally and do not load third-party scripts, ads or analytics.

Cookies and local storage

The Game does not use cookies. It uses your browser's or app's local storage only to remember your player name.

Children

The Game is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable). Players of any age should choose a nickname that does not identify them. Because the Game has no accounts, there is no personal profile that we could delete; if you believe a child's personal information has been shared, contact us.

How long we keep data

We do not run a database. The player name stays on your device until you clear the app's or browser's data. Match messages are not stored by us.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. You can remove your saved nickname at any time by clearing the Game's local storage or uninstalling the app. If you are in the EEA, UK or another region with data protection rights and think we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond.

Security

Game traffic uses encrypted connections (WSS/HTTPS). Because matches run over a public server and are not authenticated, treat everything you type in the Game as public to the players in your room.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes (for example adding ads, analytics or accounts) will be described here before they take effect.
